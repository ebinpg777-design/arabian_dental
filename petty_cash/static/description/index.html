⚙ HOW IT WORKS

Four Steps. Full Cycle.

1

Allocate

Create an allocation for a cash holder, set the limit and journal, then submit for manager approval. Once approved, the payment is disbursed and recorded.
2

Spend & Record

The cash holder logs expenses with itemized lines, vendor, expense account, and receipt attachments. Duplicate detection warns of repeat submissions automatically.
3

Approve & Post

Officers review, approve, and post expenses. Each approval creates a bill and journal entry. Small expenses below the threshold are approved automatically.
4

Settle & Close

Use the Close wizard to return remaining cash or write off small balances. The allocation is settled and archived with a complete audit trail and PDF statement.

✎ SETUP

User Guide

Petty Cash Management — Installation & Usage

Install the module — it depends on account, mail, and product, so it works with any Odoo 18 edition that includes Accounting. After installation, open Accounting › Configuration › Settings and scroll to the Petty Cash section to configure your thresholds, alert settings, receipt requirements, and duplicate detection window.

Go to Petty Cash › Journals and enable the Is Petty Cash flag on the cash or bank journals you want to use. Then navigate to Petty Cash › Allocations, click New, select the cash holder (partner), choose the petty cash journal, set the requested limit, and submit for approval. Once the manager approves, the disbursement payment is posted automatically.

Cash holders then navigate to Petty Cash › Expenses, create a new expense, select their allocation, add line items with expense accounts, attach receipts, and submit. Officers approve from the same menu or via the smart button on the allocation. When the cash holder has finished, the manager uses the Close button to launch the settlement wizard — entering the returned amount and write-off (if any) — which posts the final accounting entry and closes the allocation.
