Invoice Round Off

One click on a draft invoice or bill and 650.01 becomes 650.00: Odoo's rounding line takes the difference, booked where your accountant wants it. Or let every new customer invoice round itself off.

One click

Round Off in the invoice header

Reversible

Remove Round Off while it is draft

Standard

Odoo's own cash rounding does the books

Automatic

Every new customer invoice, if you want

WHY

Tax-inclusive prices make totals like 399.99

Odoo can round an invoice total with a cash rounding method, but on Odoo 20 a method only applies by itself when it is tied to a currency, a partner category or a payment method - and the field is hidden unless Cash Rounding is switched on. This module puts the decision where it is taken: a button on the invoice, and a company setting for the automatic version.

ℹ

Round Off / Remove Round Off

In the header of every draft invoice, credit note, receipt and vendor bill. The rounding line appears, the total becomes a whole amount, the tax totals show the rounding; the second button takes it away.
⚙

Your method

Accounting > Settings > Round Off: the rounding method the button uses - nearest unit, nearest 0.05, up, down, as a line or on the biggest tax - with its profit and loss accounts. Empty: the company's first method.
⚡

Automatic

Tick Round Off New Customer Invoices and every customer invoice is rounded off as it is created - from a sales order, by hand or by code - unless another rounding already applies to it. Bills are left alone.
