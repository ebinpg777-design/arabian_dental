Advances that manage themselves

Many vendors ask for a deposit before delivery. Odoo can pay early, but tracking which payment belongs to which order — and applying it to the right bill — is manual. This module turns that into a guided, self-settling workflow built on the accounting engine you already trust.

📝

Flexible Wizard

Size the advance as a percentage, a fixed amount, or the full remaining balance. Pick the journal, date, payment method and memo. The payment is created and posted for you.

📊

Live PO Dashboard

A colour-coded banner and smart button show the advance paid, applied to bills and still available in the order currency — clearly an advance usage badge, not the bill's payment status.

🤝

Smart Settlement

Bills are settled oldest-advance-first, partial amounts supported, across multiple bills and multiple advances — with a manual Settle Advances button as a safety net.

How it works

1

Confirm the Purchase Order and click Register Advance Payment.
2

Choose the amount, journal and date — the vendor payment is posted.
3

Create the vendor bill from the PO — advances settle automatically.
4

The bill is marked paid; any surplus stays available for later bills.

Every scenario, handled

Scenario Result
Advance larger than the billBill fully paid; surplus kept available.
Advance smaller than the billBill partially paid; balance handled normally.
Several bills from one POAdvances consumed bill by bill, oldest first.
Bill reset to draft or cancelledAuto un-reconciled; advance returns to available.
PO cancelled with open advancesChatter warning; cancel never blocked.
Currency mismatch / cash journalsDegrades gracefully; left for manual handling.

Configurable & per-company

Settings → Purchase → Advance Payments: toggle auto-settlement, cap advances to the order amount, and set a default advance journal.
